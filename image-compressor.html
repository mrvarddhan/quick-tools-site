How to Compress an Image to 20KB or 50KB Online

Many Indian government job portals such as SSC, UPSC, IBPS, and State PSC require applicants to upload passport-size photographs and signatures under strict file size limits (usually between 10KB and 50KB). Follow these simple steps to resize your photo:

  1. Upload your photo: Click the blue upload box and select your JPG or PNG image from your phone or computer.
  2. Set your target KB: Enter your desired file size (e.g., 20 for 20KB) or click one of the quick preset buttons.
  3. Compress & Download: Click Compress Image Now and download your ready-to-upload JPEG file.

Official Photo & Signature Size Guidelines

  • SSC CGL / CHSL / GD: Photo must be 20KB to 50KB (JPEG); Signature must be 10KB to 20KB.
  • UPSC Civil Services: Photo and Signature must each be between 20KB and 300KB in JPG format.
  • PAN Card (NSDL / UTI): Photo must be under 50KB (213 x 213 px); Signature must be under 50KB.
