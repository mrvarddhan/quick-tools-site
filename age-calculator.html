Why Use an Exam Cut-Off Age Calculator?

Recruitment notifications for SSC, UPSC, Railway (RRB), IBPS, and State Public Service Commissions specify an exact cut-off date (such as 1st January or 1st August) to determine minimum and maximum age eligibility. Calculating manually across leap years and 30/31-day months often leads to mistakes.

How to Calculate Your Age Online

  1. Select your Date of Birth: Enter the date printed on your Class 10th matriculation certificate.
  2. Select the Cut-Off Date: By default, today's date is selected. Change it to the official cut-off date mentioned in your exam notification.
  3. Click Calculate: Instantly view your exact age in years, months, days, and total days lived.
